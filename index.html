Shucheng Liu

Welcome to my homepage! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Statistics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Hongtu Zhu and Prof. Kai Zhang. I also work closely with Prof. Chengchun Shi. Before UNC, I received my B.S. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2025. During my undergraduate study, I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jing Zeng and Prof. Hong Zhang.

Previously, I was a large language model research intern at Tencent YouTu Lab. I was also a summer research intern at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Guanhua Chen.

My research interests include:

  • Generative modeling. Diffusion and flow-matching models and low-dimensional subspace learning from complex, high-dimensional data.
  • Multimodal foundation models. Representation learning, knowledge integration, and generalization, with applications to biomedical data.
  • Causal inference. Robust treatment effect estimation from high-dimensional observational data to support reliable decision-making.

I’m open to exploring any area that matters. I am currently seeking internship opportunities in Summer 2027.

FAST-Brain: A Flow-Aligned Spatio-Temporal Surrogate Brain Model
Shucheng Liu, Chengchun Shi, Kai Zhang, Hongtu Zhu
NeurIPS 2026

A flow-matching surrogate for rs-fMRI dynamics that regresses the clean signal instead of noise or velocity, so approximation error scales with the intrinsic rather than the ambient dimension. A history-conditioned Transformer is paired with a multi-graph GCN that fuses three anatomical priors through a learnable Laplacian polynomial.

Weighted Spline-Expanded Networks with Distributional Balancing for Continuous Treatment Effects
Shucheng Liu, Chan Park, Guanhua Chen
Under review

Model-free deconfounding for continuous treatments through distance-covariance balancing weights instead of a propensity score, combined with a spline-expanded dose-response network and an influence-function-based weighted targeted regularization that makes the estimator doubly robust. Best integrated RMSE on IHDP, News and TCGA with up to 4,000 covariates.

MoSAIC: Motion and Supervision-Aware Inference for Myocardial Scar and Edema Segmentation
Shucheng Liu, Mingcheng Hu, Liangqiao Gui, Yineng Chen, Peng Huang, Hui Guo, Shu Hu, Xin Wang, Hongtu Zhu
MICCAI CARE 2026 (Oral, top 10%)

Modality presence gates and supervision masking pool ragged multi-center cohorts with missing sequences and labels into a single model; a cine branch reads scar from motion alone, with no intensity input.

Progressive Optimization of UU-Mamba for Left Atrial Multi-Structure Segmentation in CT
Yineng Chen, Liangqiao Gui, Shucheng Liu, Mingcheng Hu, Peng Huang, Hui Guo, Shu Hu, Hongtu Zhu, Xin Wang
